05 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you may wonder.

Can I actually book a flight here?

No. BALLOON is a portfolio concept by NULLEXWEB, rebuilt from a learning project. The form demonstrates a booking flow without creating a real reservation.

Are the prices and dates real?

All prices, tour details and dates are illustrative. The next 90 days are offered as demo dates only; they do not represent flight schedules or availability.

What happens to my contact details?

Nothing is sent to a server, stored in your browser or shared. Entries exist only in this page's memory and are cleared when the demo is finished. Use sample details.

What would a real travel site need?

A verified operator, live schedules, accurate terms, weather and cancellation policies, secure contact handling, and an actual booking service. Those are outside this portfolio demo.
